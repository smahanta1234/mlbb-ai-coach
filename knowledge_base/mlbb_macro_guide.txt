MLBB Macro Strategy Guide

Objectives are more important than kills.
Teams should prioritize:
- Turtle control in early game
- Lord control in mid to late game
- Tower pressure after winning fights
- Vision and map control

Roamers should:
- provide vision
- rotate with jungler and mid laner
- secure bushes before objectives

Gold laners should:
- prioritize farming safely
- avoid unnecessary early fights
- scale into late game

EXP laners should:
- manage side lane pressure and zone during objectives
- create flank opportunities
- split push when appropriate

Junglers should:
- optimize farm rotation
- secure objectives with Retribution
- track enemy jungler movement

Mid laners should:
- rotate early
- provide wave clear
- assist side lanes

Snowball compositions should:
- force early objectives
- invade jungle
- deny enemy farm

Scaling compositions should:
- avoid risky early fights
- prioritize economy
- stall for late game power spikes
